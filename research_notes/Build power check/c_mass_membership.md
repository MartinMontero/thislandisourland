### The elected-officials table: CONTRADICTED on "Term Constraints"
- The column "Term Constraints: 2019-2029" for Ocasio-Cortez and Tlaib is wrong in kind. US House terms are two years under Article I, Section 2 of the Constitution. There is no federal term limit for House members. A "2019-2029" span is not a limit and not a current term. Their terms run in two-year congresses, and the current one ends January 3, 2027, with the new term (if re-elected) starting January 3, 2027. Rename the column "Years in office" or "Current term".
- "Cori Bush (Former) 2021-2025" and "Jamaal Bowman (Former) 2021-2025": the dates are consistent with both losing 2024 primaries (recalled, unverified; check the Missouri Secretary of State and New York State Board of Elections results).
