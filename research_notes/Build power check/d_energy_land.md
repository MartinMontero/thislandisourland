## 1. 50 GW / LBNL 74-132 GW -- CONFIRMED WITH CORRECTION (partial)
- Verified (energy.gov, DOE release dated Dec. 20, 2024, read 2026-11-17, https://www.energy.gov/articles/doe-releases-new-report-evaluating-increase-electricity-demand-data-centers): data centers used 176 TWh in 2023, about 4.4% of US electricity; the 2028 projection is 325-580 TWh, about 6.7% to 12%. The page uses TWh only and never mentions GW. It links the report: https://eta-publications.lbl.gov/sites/default/files/2024-12/lbnl-2024-united-states-data-center-energy-usage-report.pdf (title given there: "2024 Report on U.S. Data Center Energy Use", LBNL).
- NOT VERIFIED: whether 74-132 GW appears inside the LBNL report. I could not read the PDF. The DOE page has no GW figures, and press coverage of the report is framed in TWh and percent. Working assumption (unconfirmed): 74-132 GW is someone's conversion of 325-580 TWh (325,000 GWh / 8,760 h = 37 GW average; 580,000 / 8,760 = 66 GW average, so 74-132 does not match a simple average-load conversion either). Do not attribute 74-132 GW to LBNL unless the PDF is found to say it.
- NOT VERIFIED: the "50 GW for AI by 2028" source. Pointer only: a search surfaced Anthropic's "Build AI in America" (anthropic.com/news/build-ai-in-america) and a related Anthropic PDF, plus a RAND brief (RBA3845-1) -- unread. Do not attribute until read.

## 7. NEPA / permitting / federal land for data centers -- CONFIRMED (energy.gov items only)
- July 23-24, 2025: DOE announced four sites for AI data centers and energy generation on federal land: Idaho National Laboratory, Oak Ridge Reservation, Paducah, Savannah River Site. It cited three executive orders, including "Accelerating Federal Permitting of Data Center Infrastructure." The page does not mention NEPA. (https://www.energy.gov/articles/doe-announces-site-selection-ai-data-center-and-energy-infrastructure-development-federal, read 2026-11-17)
- July 20, 2026: NNSA selected Amentum to negotiate a 1 GW data center with about 2 GW of on-site generation (natural gas bridging to nuclear) on a proposed phased lease at Savannah River Site, SC; selection is not a final lease and depends on permitting and other approvals. (https://www.energy.gov/nnsa/articles/nnsa-selects-amentum-ai-data-center-and-energy-project-savannah-river-site, read 2026-11-17)
- July 29, 2026: DOE announced a Paducah, KY partnership: Brookfield, NextEra, Big Rivers Electric, Jackson Purchase Energy Cooperative and Paducah Power System; over $100 billion private investment; 1.8 GW campus, about 2 GW new gas, up to 2.6 GW batteries; completion expected 2031; the power service agreement needs Kentucky PSC approval. (https://www.energy.gov/articles/energy-department-announces-partnership-expand-reliable-affordable-energy-access-and-power, read 2026-11-17)
- NOT VERIFIED: the NEPA categorical-exclusion actions themselves (executive order text/dates, Interior actions, permitting statutes). The report's phrase is described as "industry calls"; the federal actions above are real but none of those pages mention NEPA.
