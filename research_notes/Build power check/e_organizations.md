Legal status labelled 'believed' is from general knowledge and needs an IRS check before publishing. ## What each does and how to take part

- **Labor Notes**: Independent media and training project that teaches rank-and-file workers how to organize at work, run union campaigns and reform their unions. Take part: join, train, donate, find. Notes: Conference attendance was reported at about four thousand by one outlet (internationalviewpoint.org result title); not independently counted. Editorial line is pro-union and left-leaning. Sources: https://labornotes.org/2026; https://labornotes.org/2026/07/learning-recharging-uniting-labor-notes-2026; https://www.thenation.com/article/economy/labor-notes-conference-ai-unions/
- **AFL-CIO**: The US federation of 60-plus unions; runs organizing campaigns, a union-finder and join pages, and community programs including Working America. Take part: join, find, donate, volunteer. Notes: Working America (AFL-CIO community affiliate for non-union members) was checked separately and dropped for lack of a dated 2026 item. Also an electoral actor through its political arm. Sources: https://aflcio.org/press/releases/despite-relentless-attacks-nearly-half-million-workers-unionized-2025; https://www.commondreams.org/newswire/despite-relentless-attacks-nearly-half-a-million-workers-unionized-in-2025; https://minnesotareformer.com/2026/06/07/afl-cio-president-aims-to-unionize-at-least-2-million-workers-in-5-years/; https://aflcio.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/2026%20EC%20Report%20Convention%20%281%29.pdf
- **UE (United Electrical, Radio and Machine Workers of America)**: Independent, member-run union with an organizing program and steward/organizer workshops; takes workers from any industry. Take part: join, train, find. Notes: Small relative to SEIU/Teamsters. SEIU, Teamsters and CWA join/organize pages were checked: CWA has a 2026 member publication but all three were cut for the 35-entry cap or lack of a dated 2026 item. Sources: https://www.ueunion.org/ue-news/2026/locals-228-and-728-hold-joint-steward-organizer-workshop; https://www.ue-easternregion.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/ER-Newsletter-Spring-2026.pdf
- **Workers Defense Project**: Texas worker center that helps low-wage and construction workers recover unpaid wages, win safety protections and organize. Take part: get_help, join, volunteer, donate. Notes: Freedom Inc (Madison worker-center style group) was dropped; see dropped list. Sources: https://workersdefense.org/en/; https://www.guidestar.org/profile/35-2296166; https://m.stylemagazine.com/news/2026/jan/29/christian-menefee-earns-workers-defense-action-fund-endorsement-ahead-of-tx-18-runoff-amid-immigration-concerns

Notes: Check current NLRB quorum and case-processing status before relying on it: not verified here. Link the live NLRB page rather than a PDF. Sources: https://www.nlrb.gov/sites/default/files/attachments/pages/node-9545/235351_steps_to_forming_a_union_pun.pdf; https://www.workcenter.gov/step-by-step-guide; https://www.nlrb.gov/case/16-CA-398637
- **Project Equity**: Nonprofit that helps retiring business owners sell to their employees (employee ownership) and trains advisors. Take part: get_help, use_tool, donate, find. Notes: Seed Commons (co-op conversion lender) was dropped: only a 2025 impact report surfaced. Sources: https://project-equity.org/services/; https://project-equity.org/?p=265394; https://eohub.rutgers.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Decision-Guide-for-Owners-Primer-OI-08.03.26.pdf

Notes: Brand changed from PICO National Network; affiliates use varied names. Directory of local affiliates should be checked per state.
