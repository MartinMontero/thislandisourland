- Prior knowledge (confirm before publishing): the 2025 discharge petition's Republican signers were Thomas Massie, Marjorie Taylor Greene, Lauren Boebert and Nancy Mace (4 Republicans; all others Democrats); Adelita Grijalva was sworn in Nov 12, 2025 as the 218th signature; Trump signed the act Nov 19, 2025.
