## Q2. Lesley Groff: the American Express Centurion "decoy flight"

### Takeaway
That Groff used the term "decoy flight", that Amex bookings were involved, and that the committee asked DOJ to investigate her further are all CONFIRMED. Two details in the file are WRONG. First, Groff testified the decoy flight "was not for a visa", the opposite of what the file says. Second, the source found has the routes the other way round: the fake ticket was Rome–London. The file's direct quotes of the committee's credibility finding could NOT BE VERIFIED.

### Cited Findings
- **Groff's interview: CONFIRMED.** She gave a closed-door transcribed interview to House Oversight on June 9, 2026. The transcript was released in late June. She was Epstein's executive assistant from 2001 to 2019. — [WSJ via To Vima, June 25, 2026](https://www.tovima.com/wsj/see-what-jeffrey-epsteins-longtime-secretary-told-congress/). The file calls it a "deposition"; it was a transcribed interview. Minor point.
- **She called a trip a "decoy flight": CONFIRMED.** Her explanation was that the woman "did not want to tell her parents that she was going to take this flight." — [WSJ via To Vima, June 25, 2026](https://www.tovima.com/wsj/see-what-jeffrey-epsteins-longtime-secretary-told-congress/)
- **The file says Groff "claimed this was merely to help the woman obtain a visa": WRONG.** Groff testified the decoy flight "was not for a visa." — [WSJ via To Vima, June 25, 2026](https://www.tovima.com/wsj/see-what-jeffrey-epsteins-longtime-secretary-told-congress/). Separately, a January 2016 Amex email from "Epstein's assistant" said the traveller "needs to show an itinerary for this flight." The aviation blog that reported it frames that request as visa-related. — [View from the Wing, 2026](https://viewfromthewing.com/jeffrey-epstein-had-17-amex-cards-and-11-million-points-his-black-card-concierge-booked-decoy-visa-flights/). So the document trail points to a visa purpose, and Groff denied it.
- **Routes: CONTESTED, and the file is likely reversed.** The file says the fake itinerary was Rome–Miami and the secret real ticket was to London. The documents reported say the reverse: Epstein's assistant asked Amex for a Rome-to-London flight "never meant to be taken." It was booked around the same time as a Rome-to-Miami flight. The email reads "This is a decoy flight" and says the traveller "will not really take it." — [View from the Wing](https://viewfromthewing.com/jeffrey-epstein-had-17-amex-cards-and-11-million-points-his-black-card-concierge-booked-decoy-visa-flights/), summarising [Bloomberg, May 11, 2026](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-05-11/epstein-used-american-express-black-card-to-book-fake-flights-for-women). The file's broader claim that Amex "facilitated the logistical movement of victims" goes further than this wording.
- **Recommendation on Groff: CONFIRMED.** DOJ should "further question and investigate" her possible knowledge of and role in Epstein's crimes. — [House Oversight](https://oversight.house.gov/release/house-oversight-committee-releases-report-on-the-federal-governments-mishandling-of-epstein-and-maxwell-criminal-investigations/?source=pmbug.com); [Fox News, Nov 10, 2026](https://foxnews.com/politics/explosive-house-report-exposes-failures-says-allowed-epsteins-abuse-continue-years)

This comes from search-result headlines; the articles were not read. — [Mediaite](https://www.mediaite.com/media/news/shes-lying-epstein-victims-accuse-his-ex-assistant-of-misleading-congress-with-false-information/); [KRDO/CNN, July 17, 2026](https://krdo.com/news/2026/07/17/exclusive-congress-discussing-affidavits-from-epstein-survivors-about-lesley-groff-as-survivors-told-cnn-she-lied/)
