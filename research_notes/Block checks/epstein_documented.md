# Fact-check: block id="elite-impunity", column "Documented by official bodies or court records"
Checked 2026-11-14 against original sources opened with WebFetch/WebSearch. Quotes kept to 14 words or fewer.

## 1. The 2008 deal -- VERDICT: PARTLY (substance confirmed; label and one phrase need tightening)
Block: "Jeffrey Epstein avoided federal charges under a non-prosecution agreement and pleaded guilty in state court to solicitation of prostitution and procuring a minor for prostitution. He was sentenced to 18 months and served under 13, with work release. Victims were not told for nearly a year, and the 11th Circuit said they were left in the dark. The same court held 7 to 4 that, with no charge filed, they could not enforce their rights."
Sources opened:
- https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/ca11/19-13843/19-13843-2021-04-15.html (In re Wild, en banc, Apr 15, 2021)
- https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/press-release/file/1336416/dl?inline= (OPR executive summary)
Confirmed: plea June 30, 2008 to "solicitation of prostitution" and "procuring a person under the age of 18 for prostitution" (opinion/dissent text). OPR: state court imposed 12 + 6 months consecutive; work release from Oct 2008; "less than 13 months" served. Opinion says victims "left in the dark." Majority: Newsom plus Pryor, Wilson, Lagoa, Brasher, Tjoflat in full, Luck in part = 7; dissent Branch, Martin, Jill Pryor, Hull = 4; Jordan, Rosenbaum, Grant recused. Holding: "Because the government never filed charges against Epstein" there was no proceeding in which to seek relief.
Discrepancies:
a) Heading "The 2008 deal": the NPA was signed Sept 24, 2007; the plea and sentence were June 30, 2008. Suggest "The 2007 agreement and 2008 plea" (or "The Epstein plea deal").
b) "Victims were not told for nearly a year": the opinion's dissent says the victims were not notified of the signed agreement (Sept 2007); Wild learned of it from a July 9, 2008 filing (about 9.5 months); victims got a copy in Aug 2008 under court order (about 11 months). "Nearly a year" is a loose round-up. Suggested: "Victims were not told of the agreement until July 2008, about ten months after it was signed."
c) "7 to 4": Judge Luck joined only parts of the majority opinion. The 7-4 count is the usual reporting; fine if kept, but the majority did NOT decide whether CVRA rights attach pre-charge. It held there is no stand-alone private right to sue outside an existing proceeding. Suggested: "...held 7 to 4 that, with no charge filed, they had no proceeding in which to enforce their rights."
d) "Left in the dark" was written by the same (majority) opinion, but as an expression of sympathy; the block's attribution to "the 11th Circuit" is fair.

## 3. The jail (OIG 23-085) -- VERDICT: CONFIRMED (one wording point on Baden)
Block: guards missed checks and counts and falsified records; about half of cameras not recording after July 29, 2019 disk failure; off suicide watch July 24; two guards in DPAs, charges dismissed; ME ruled suicide; OIG found nothing contradicting FBI no-crime finding; pathologist paid by Epstein's brother said injuries pointed more to murder.
Sources opened:
- https://oig.justice.gov/sites/default/files/reports/23-085.pdf (OIG report 23-085, June 2023; first 100,000 characters read)
- https://www.cbsnews.com/news/did-jeffrey-epstein-kill-himself-60-minutes-investigates-2020-01-05/
Confirmed: June 2023 report; no counts after about 4 p.m. and no 30-minute rounds after about 10:40 p.m. on Aug 9, 2019, with records falsely showing completion; Noel and Thomas falsely certified records; DVR 2 failure covering roughly half the cameras (staff learned Aug 8); removal from suicide watch July 24, 2019; DPAs entered May 25, 2021, all charges dismissed Dec 13, 2021; OIG "did not uncover evidence contradicting" the FBI finding; ME ruled suicide by hanging. CBS: Baden observed the autopsy for brother Mark, who pays him.
Discrepancies:
a) Block says the guards "missed required checks and counts and falsified records" -- accurate, but it was the two guards (Noel, Thomas) for the falsification; OIG also said other staff falsified records and were not prosecuted. Optional.
b) The July 29 failure: OIG says the disk failure was on DVR 2 and staff did not learn of it until Aug 8. The block's "about half" is OIG's "roughly half." Fine.
c) Baden: CBS (60 Minutes, Jan 2020) says he "leans toward homicide" and stops short of a final conclusion; the block's "pointed more to murder" matches in substance. Suggested exact framing: "said the evidence leaned toward homicide."

d) "we found no Justice Department figure after January 30": DOJ's Feb 14, 2026 Section 3 report (https://www.justice.gov/opa/media/1434856/dl) has no page counts, consistent with the block. e) Not verified: 427-1 roll call on clerk.house.gov (not opened); NPR/AP report used.
