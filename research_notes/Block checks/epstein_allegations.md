## 1. Trump and Blanche -- WRONG (title) / PARTLY (wording of allegations)
Block: "House Judiciary Committee Democrats (ranking member Raskin) released a report on October 26, 2026 alleging that President Trump was an active participant and that Deputy Attorney General Todd Blanche obstructed. The Oversight Republicans' report concludes Trump is not implicated. Trump and the White House deny the allegations."

Also https://spectrumnews1.com/ky/louisville/politics/2026/10/26/trump-epstein-house-judiciary-committee-democrats-report
- "Active participant": appears in press coverage of the report, not in the press release page. IBTimes quotes the report: "an active participant in Jeffrey Epstein's sexual abuse of young women" (https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/house-democrats-special-counsel-trump-epstein-allegations-1828022). Press release wording is "may have been co-conspirators". Block should hedge.
- Blanche title: WRONG. Blanche is Attorney General. Justice Department bio says confirmed Aug 8, 2026 as 88th AG (https://www.justice.gov/dag/bio/deputy-attorney-general-todd-blanche). Senate confirmed 50-49 early Aug 8, 2026 (https://www.michiganpublic.org/2026-08-08/senate-votes-to-confirm-todd-blanche-as-attorney-general); he had been acting AG after Bondi was pushed out (April 2026, https://wtop.com/national/2026/04/a-look-at-todd-blanche-the-ex-trump-lawyer-whos-the-presidents-pick-for-acting-attorney-general/). The alleged conduct (July 2025 Maxwell prison interview) occurred while he was Deputy AG. Oct 26 coverage already calls him Attorney General.
- "Obstructed": report language is that he "may have engaged in criminal obstruction of justice" (IBTimes) / "witness tampering and obstruction of an official proceeding" (Common Dreams), based on the July 2025 Maxwell interview. Block states it as obstruction having occurred; it is an allegation.
- Oversight Republicans' report: CONFIRMED. Released Nov 10, 2026; says the committee "did not identify any documents that would implicate President Trump" in Epstein/Maxwell crimes. https://oversight.house.gov/release/house-oversight-committee-releases-report-on-the-federal-governments-mishandling-of-epstein-and-maxwell-criminal-investigations/
- Denial: White House spokeswoman Lauren Bis: "None of these allegations are true"; Trump "fully exonerated" (Spectrum, IBTimes). A DOJ spokesperson called the report a "last-ditch effort" (IBTimes). Block omits Blanche/DOJ response; the block's own rule is to show the named person's response.
- Correction wording: "...alleging that President Trump may have been a co-conspirator and an active participant in Epstein's abuse in the 1990s, and that Todd Blanche, now Attorney General and then Deputy Attorney General, may have engaged in witness tampering and obstruction in a July 2025 interview with Ghislaine Maxwell. The White House says none of the allegations are true, and a Justice Department spokesperson called the report a last-ditch effort by Democrats."
