# Barrack, Nader, Broidy, Qatar blockade and Tillerson claims: independent source check (as of 2026-11-14)

Method note: I did not read anything in "Saudi Trump financial ties check/". Pages were opened with WebFetch, which returns a model-written extract of each page, not raw text. Short quotes below are as relayed by that extract; none was checked against a raw page view. "Primary" means a DOJ release or similar official page. No court docket, indictment PDF, verdict form or plea agreement was opened for any claim.

## Claim 1. Barrack: indicted July 2021 as unregistered UAE agent; acquitted on every count on Nov 4, 2022

### Takeaway
Verdict: CONFIRMED (indictment, primary) and CONFIRMED-SECONDARY (acquittal on all counts, Nov 4, 2022). The block understates the charges: they also included obstruction and false statements. The count number is 7 per DOJ, but one outlet says 9.

### Cited Findings
- EDNY/DOJ release dated July 20, 2021: "A seven-count indictment was unsealed today in federal court in Brooklyn." Charges: acting and conspiring to act as UAE agents (Apr 2016 to Apr 2018), plus obstruction and false statements against Barrack. E.D.N.Y. No. 21-CR-371. — [DOJ EDNY](https://www.justice.gov/usao-edny/pr/former-advisor-presidential-candidate-among-three-defendants-charged-acting-agents)
- The release says Barrack and Grimes were arrested in California that morning. It does not give the grand jury's return date. — same source
- Acquittal: "acquitted of all counts Friday" (PBS/AP, dated Nov 4, 2022, a Friday). The jury deliberated three days. Charges listed: unregistered foreign agent, obstruction, false statements. — [PBS/AP](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/jury-finds-trump-ally-tom-barrack-not-guilty-of-foreign-agent-charges)
- Fortune: acquitted of all counts on Friday, Nov 4, 2022. Matthew Grimes was also acquitted. — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2022/11/04/tom-barrack-acquitted-all-charges-donald-trump-inauguration-emirates/)
- Commercial Observer says "seven-count indictment" for Barrack, with not guilty on all charges. — [Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/2022/11/thomas-barrack-acquitted/)
- Conflict: Courthouse News says Barrack "faced nine counts." — [Courthouse News](https://www.courthousenews.com/trump-ally-roundly-acquitted-in-foreign-lobbying-trial/). This conflicts with DOJ's seven. The seven is probably the indictment total. Whether the trial total differed (for example after a superseding indictment) is not established.

### Inferences
- The block's "every count" matches all sources. The number of counts is safest left out.
- "Indicted in July 2021" is safe in the sense of announced and unsealed July 20, 2021. The grand jury vote date was not verified.

### Gaps
- Not opened: the verdict form, the indictment PDF, the docket (CourtListener refused automated access), and Reuters via StreetInsider (fetch failed). The CNN version (abc17news) was blocked by robots.txt.
- The 7 versus 9 count discrepancy is unresolved.

### Proposed wording
"Tom Barrack was charged in a seven-count indictment unsealed July 20, 2021, with acting as an unregistered agent of the UAE, obstruction and false statements, and was acquitted on every count on November 4, 2022."

## Claim 2. Nader pleaded guilty to conspiracy to defraud the US over concealed foreign contributions

### Takeaway
Verdict: CONFIRMED-SECONDARY for the plea (single count of felony conspiracy to defraud the US). The sentence is CONFIRMED (primary). The plea date and court were not verified from a primary source.

### Cited Findings
- Intercept, Jan 16, 2022: single count of "felony conspiracy to defraud the U.S. government." "Nader's plea has not been previously reported." Plea paperwork was filed in the prior month (about December 2021). A December sentencing memo said he had agreed to plead "months earlier." Prosecutors sought five years. — [The Intercept](https://theintercept.com/2022/01/16/uae-2016-election-trump-clinton-george-nader/)
- DOJ sentencing release, July 18, 2023: Nader "sentenced today" to an additional 1 year and 8 months (20 months), to start after his five-year E.D. Va. term on unrelated charges. The release says he conspired with Ahmad "Andy" Khawaja to transfer $4.9 million to fund unlawful contributions to unwitting political committees, with at least $3.5 million funneled in connection with the 2016 election. It does not state the plea count or date, and the extract showed no court. — [DOJ](https://justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/businessman-sentenced-35m-foreign-conduit-contribution-scheme)
- The underlying case was indicted by a D.C. grand jury on Nov 7, 2019 (unsealed Dec 3, 2019). The DOJ release says Nader was charged with "conspiring with Khawaja to make conduit campaign contributions, and related offenses." — [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/california-ceo-and-seven-others-charged-multi-million-dollar-conduit-campaign-contribution)
- Wikipedia (tertiary): plea in 2021; sentencing transcript cited from D.D.C., Judge Randolph D. Moss; 20 months. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Nader_(businessman))

### Inferences
- The court is almost certainly D.D.C., but no source I opened states it for the plea itself.
- "Concealed foreign contributions" is a fair paraphrase. DOJ frames it as foreign-funded conduit contributions that disguised the true source.

### Gaps
- The plea date, plea court and plea agreement were not obtained. The DOJ release of the plea was not found, and CourtListener was blocked.

### Proposed wording
"George Nader pleaded guilty (reported January 2022) to one count of conspiracy to defraud the United States over foreign-funded conduit campaign contributions, and was sentenced on July 18, 2023 to 20 months, consecutive to a five-year sentence on separate charges."

## Claim 3. Broidy pleaded guilty to conspiracy to violate FARA over lobbying for Jho Low; pardoned January 2021

### Takeaway
Verdict: CONFIRMED (plea, primary). The Jho Low link and the pardon are CONFIRMED-SECONDARY. The block omits that the scheme also served a Chinese government aim.

### Cited Findings
- DOJ release: Broidy pleaded guilty to "one count of conspiracy to violate the Foreign Agents Registration Act," with a $6.6 million forfeiture. Judge Kollar-Kotelly is named. The release says "today" and gives no date. It refers only to "Foreign National A," not Jho Low by name. — [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/elliott-broidy-pleads-guilty-back-channel-lobbying-campaign-drop-1mdb-investigation-and)
- DOJ OIG release: pleaded guilty Oct 20, 2020, in D.D.C., to conspiring to serve as an unregistered foreign agent. The OIG release gives no count number and does not name Jho Low. The effort ran about March 2017 to January 2018 and was also on behalf of the PRC. — [DOJ OIG](https://oig.justice.gov/news/press-release/non-doj-individual-pleaded-guilty-conspiracy-serve-unregistered-agent-foreign)
- Jho Low named, with Broidy admitting $9 million from him: [FARA.us (Caplin & Drysdale)](https://www.fara.us/elliott-broidy-fara-guilty-plea); [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2021/01/20/trump-elliott-broidy-pardon) (lobbying DOJ to stop seizing Low's assets; also pushing the extradition of Guo Wengui).
- Pardon: on Trump's last full day, Jan 19, 2021 (Wikipedia list); reported Jan 20, 2021 by Axios and NBC as part of the final-hours list. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_people_granted_executive_clemency_in_the_first_Trump_presidency); [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/donald-trump/full-list-trump-s-last-minute-pardons-commuted-sentences-n1254806)

### Inferences
- "Lobbying for Jho Low" is accurate but incomplete. The plea also covered lobbying for a PRC aim (the return of Guo Wengui), per DOJ OIG and Axios.

### Gaps
- Not opened: the DOJ Office of the Pardon Attorney grant page (fetch failed), the White House list, the plea agreement and the docket. The pardon date rests on secondary sources.

### Proposed wording
"Elliott Broidy pleaded guilty on October 20, 2020 to one count of conspiracy to violate the Foreign Agents Registration Act, over back-channel lobbying for Jho Low and the Chinese government, and was pardoned on January 19, 2021."

## Claim 4. Blockade began June 5, 2017; documented Oval Office contacts by Broidy and Nader are October to December 2017

### Takeaway
Verdict: blockade date CONFIRMED-SECONDARY. The "Oval Office contacts by Broidy and Nader … October to December 2017" wording is partly OVERSTATED. Only one Oval Office meeting (Broidy, Oct 6, 2017) is documented in what I read. Nader's documented Trump contact is a photo at a Dallas fundraiser.

### Cited Findings
- Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Bahrain and Egypt severed ties with Qatar on June 5, 2017, alongside the air, sea and land blockade. — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2017/6/17/infographic-qatar-diplomatic-crisis); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2018/3/6/emails-show-uae-linked-lobbying-effort-to-oust-tillerson-reports)
- Broidy's Oval Office audience: Oct 6, 2017, 3:45 p.m. Broidy briefed Trump alone. Nader had written him a script. — [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/politics/politics-features/elliott-broidy-donald-trump-swamp-793159/); [AP via Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/the-princes-the-president-and-the-fortune-seekers/); [AP via WHYY](https://whyy.org/articles/the-princes-the-president-and-the-fortune-seekers) (names the Oval Office).
- Oct 25, 2017: Nader photographed with Trump backstage at an RNC fundraiser in Dallas that Broidy co-hosted, not in the Oval Office. — AP via Times of Israel (above)
- Dec 2, 2017: Broidy met Trump again. The extract gave no location and no sign that Nader attended. — AP via Times of Israel; WHYY (location not stated)
- Broidy's Qatar pressure strategy predated the blockade: finalized late March 2017, with a May 2017 Fairmont conference. — [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/politics/politics-features/elliott-broidy-donald-trump-swamp-793159/)

### Inferences
- The point that late-2017 contacts cannot have caused a June 2017 blockade holds on dates. But Broidy and Nader's Qatar advocacy began months before June 5, 2017, so the block's phrasing could be read as saying they did nothing earlier.
- The AP and Rolling Stone accounts rely on the leaked Broidy emails, which are not independently authenticated by anything I opened. Nothing I opened contradicts them.

### Gaps
- No source opened places Nader in the Oval Office in Oct to Dec 2017. The location of the Dec 2, 2017 meeting was not found.
- The AP original on the AP site, Washington Times AP blockade story (fetch failed) and the NBC copy of the AP investigation (robots) were not opened.

### Proposed wording
"The blockade of Qatar began on June 5, 2017. The documented Trump contacts of Broidy and Nader come later: Broidy's Oval Office meeting on October 6, 2017, Nader's photo with Trump at a Dallas fundraiser on October 25, and a further Broidy meeting on December 2. Their advocacy on Qatar did start earlier in 2017, but nothing read shows it caused the blockade."

## Claim 5. Mnuchin testified at Barrack's trial that Barrack's position was clearly in support of Qatar

### Takeaway
Verdict: CONFIRMED-SECONDARY (two independent outlets quote it). One fix: Mnuchin was the former Treasury Secretary by then, and he testified for the defense on Oct 20, 2022.

### Cited Findings
- "His position was clearly in support of Qatar," Mnuchin testified. Barrack called Trump's backing of the blockade a mistake. The conversation was in June 2017. — [Reuters via Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/1-mnuchin-takes-stand-trump-181251099.html); [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/former-treasury-secretary-steven-mnuchin-testifies-trump-confidant-thomas-barrack)
- CBS places the testimony on Oct 20, 2022, in a meeting the day after the blockade was enacted. — CBS

### Inferences
- The block's placement of this under "what the dates do not support" is sensible. It helps Barrack's defense, and it shows he lobbied against the blockade-aligned UAE position. It does not say he never acted for the UAE, and the jury acquitted anyway.

### Gaps
- No transcript read. The quote comes from news summaries.

### Proposed wording
"At Barrack's trial, former Treasury Secretary Steven Mnuchin, a defense witness, testified that Barrack's position on the blockade was 'clearly in support of Qatar.'"

## Claim 6. Whether Broidy and Nader caused Tillerson's firing is contested: Nader urged it; Trump gave other reasons (March 2018)

### Takeaway
Verdict: CONFIRMED-SECONDARY. Both halves hold. Add that Broidy urged it too, and give the dates.

### Cited Findings
- NYT (Mar 21, 2018, via Al Jazeera): Nader and Broidy pushed the White House to fire Tillerson. The coverage reports the lobbying and the firing as separate developments and does not say the lobbying caused the dismissal. — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2018/3/22/uae-adviser-lobbied-trump-to-oust-tillerson-nyt)
- Emails (BBC via Al Jazeera, Mar 6, 2018): Broidy said Tillerson "performing poorly," called him "a tower of Jello." — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2018/3/6/emails-show-uae-linked-lobbying-effort-to-oust-tillerson-reports)
- Oct 6, 2017: Broidy told Trump to fire Tillerson at a "politically convenient time." — [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/politics/politics-features/elliott-broidy-donald-trump-swamp-793159/)
- Tillerson was fired Mar 13, 2018. Trump cited disagreements, mainly the Iran deal: "we disagreed on things." — [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/trump-says-decision-to-oust-tillerson-was-made-by-myself); [AOL/AP](https://www.aol.com/article/news/2018/03/13/trump-says-he-fired-tillerson-due-to-a-disagreement-over-the-iran-deal/23384410/)
- Other reasons reported: Paris accord, Asia-Pacific trade, and Jerusalem embassy disagreements. — [CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/news/rex-tillerson-fired-secretary-of-state-today-2018-03-13)
- The Nader and Broidy lobbying reports (Mar 6 and Mar 21, 2018) came out after or around the firing, so the reporting itself is about attempts, not outcomes.

### Inferences
- "Contested" is an accurate characterization: attempts documented, causation not shown, and Trump stated different reasons.

### Gaps
- NYT original not opened. No source I read says the lobbying did or did not influence Trump.

### Proposed wording
"Broidy and Nader both lobbied for Tillerson's removal in 2017 and early 2018 (reported by the NYT in March 2018). Whether that caused his firing on March 13, 2018 is not shown. Trump cited policy disagreements, chiefly the Iran deal."
