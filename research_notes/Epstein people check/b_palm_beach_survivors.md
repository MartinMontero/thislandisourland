## Corrections to the brief (discrepancies)
1. Det. Joe Recarey: "died 2016?" is WRONG. Obituary says died May 25, 2018, age 50, "after a brief illness"; retired from Palm Beach PD in 2013. (Dignity Memorial obituary; one source, no second source checked.)
2. Alfredo Rodriguez "2009 conviction": 2009 is the arrest (Nov 3, 2009 FBI sting, per newsletter). Plea and sentence date CONTESTED/NOT VERIFIED: the newsletter says plea 2011, 18 months, served 13. I could not reach a court docket or news report. (My unchecked recollection is 2010; do not use it.) Death Dec 28, 2014 of mesothelioma, age 60: single secondary source, NOT VERIFIED.
3. In re Wild en banc did NOT hold that CVRA rights attach only after charges. It said it "needn't decide" that question and denied mandamus because the CVRA gives no private right of action outside an existing proceeding. Any note saying en banc "held rights don't attach pre-charge" is WRONG.
4. "Perversion of Justice" parts: Hillman Foundation and UW Shadid page both say three parts. Wikipedia's Julie K. Brown text (as summarised) says "five-part". CONTESTED; use three unless the Herald index shows otherwise.
5. Polk year: the award is the 70th Polk Awards, honouring 2018 work, ceremony April 5, 2019. Say "2018 George Polk Award" or "awarded in 2019" with that explanation. Her Pulitzer is a 2026 special citation, not a prize.
6. "53-page indictment": Marra opinion says prosecutors drafted an 82-page prosecution memo and a 53-page indictment by May 2007. The OPR text I could read says a 60-count draft indictment, no page counts. Compatible, not contradictory.
7. "Potential life sentence": not found in Marra opinion or the OPR excerpts read. NOT VERIFIED.
8. Reiter's inquiry start: Wikipedia says 2004; Marra and OPR say the 2005 complaint from a 14-year-old's parents. Prefer 2005.
