## 2. Alessi and Rodriguez

- Juan Alessi, 2005 statement naming Maxwell: CONFIRMED. PBPD report narrative: on Nov 21, 2005 Juan and Maria Alessi were interviewed at the State Attorney's Office with attorney Donald Morrell present; Alessi said he first dealt with "Ms. Maxwell originally", described as his girlfriend, before dealing with Epstein directly. Just Security's timeline (a second source) calls it the "Sworn Statement of Juan P. Alessi (PBPD)" dated Nov 21, 2005, saying Maxwell was Epstein's "girlfriend" and the staff's immediate superior. He was employed eleven years as house manager, driver and maintenance (PBPD report; probable cause affidavit).
- Name discrepancy: the OCR of the probable cause affidavit renders him "Jose Alessi"; the PBPD narratives say "Juan Alessi" (spelled "Aless" in one line); Just Security says "Juan P. Alessi". Use Juan.
- "Sworn": REPORTED. Just Security says sworn; the PBPD report mirror says interviewed. The affidavit says taped sworn statements were taken from "five victims and seventeen witnesses." I did not see the transcript itself.
- Alfredo Rodriguez, 2005-06 police contact: CONFIRMED. PBPD report: Nov 9, 2005 detectives went to his Miami home, he was out; Nov 10 he phoned and said he worked for Epstein about six months; Dec 20, 2005 a subpoena was sought because he had eluded process servers; Jan 5, 2006 sworn taped statement at the State Attorney's Office; Jan 6, 2006 he handed over a green folder of documents and notes in a Boca Raton bank parking lot.
- Rodriguez, 2009 sting and charge: CONFIRMED. DOJ filings (mirrored on efta-search.vercel.app) show an Information filed Jan 29, 2010, Case 10-80015-CR (Marra), one count of obstruction, 18 U.S.C. 1512(c), for conduct from Jan 18, 2007 to Nov 3, 2009. Nov 3, 2009 is also the date of the FBI sting meeting (LADbible).
- Rodriguez, plea date: NOT VERIFIED. Mirrors show an unsigned plea agreement with blank dates. Secondary sources conflict: Debrief says 2011; Epstein Wiki says only "eventually pleaded guilty." The case number (10-) and the Jan 2010 Information make 2011 unlikely.
- Rodriguez, sentence: REPORTED, length consistent. 18 months in federal prison (LADbible, Debrief, Epstein Wiki, epsteinrevealed). Debrief adds he served 13 months.
- Rodriguez, sentencing date: CONTESTED. Epstein Wiki: June 18, 2010. LADbible: April 2012. Debrief and epsteinrevealed: 2011. No docket entry was found. Treat as unresolved; pull the docket from PACER or CourtListener (S.D. Fla. 9:10-cr-80015).
- Rodriguez, death: REPORTED. Dec 28, 2014, age 60, mesothelioma (Debrief; Epstein Wiki citing a funeral-home obituary, and explicitly correcting an earlier "2015" claim). LADbible says 2015, no exact date. So "2015" is WRONG if the obituary is right, but I did not open the obituary itself.

## 4. Michael Baden

- Oct 30, 2019: CONFIRMED. On Fox & Friends (Wednesday Oct 30, 2019; NPR, KSAT/AP, Fox5NY) Baden said the injuries were "more indicative of homicidal strangulation" than suicide (Mediaite quote), citing fractures of the hyoid and thyroid cartilage / larynx, and called them "extremely unusual in suicidal hangings" (AP via KSAT). AP also has him saying his observations were not conclusive. One AP photo caption says Oct 29; the article text says Wednesday.
- Jan 5, 2020, CBS 60 Minutes: CONFIRMED. He also said he hesitates to give a final opinion until all the evidence is in (fetcher paraphrase of a longer sentence). So the accurate characterization is: strongly leaning homicide, not a definitive finding.
- NYC Chief Medical Examiner Barbara Sampson's position (Oct 30, 2019, Fox5NY, NPR): "The cause is hanging, the manner is suicide"; no reason for a second investigation.
- Who hired him: CONFIRMED. Epstein's brother, Mark Epstein (CBS: he observed the four-hour autopsy on behalf of Mark; KSAT/AP: hired by Mark; NPR: hired by the family).
- Who paid him: REPORTED, weak. The CBS page (per fetcher) has Baden acknowledging he is "being paid by Epstein's brother." NPR, Mediaite, and KSAT/AP do not mention payment. I could not tell whether that is Baden's own quote or CBS narration; verify against the CBS transcript.

- "Poor judgment" on the NPA decision: CONFIRMED. Sentence: Acosta's decision to resolve the federal investigation through the NPA "constitutes poor judgment" (Executive Summary Section V.A). Location: archive.org OCR page_000011 (PDF page 12), whose final text block is the folio "x". So printed page x of the Executive Summary. The same section says OPR did not find professional misconduct on the NPA decision (page x) and found no evidence of corruption.
- Victim notification: CONFIRMED. Acosta "exercised poor judgment when he failed to make certain that the state" would notify victims of the state plea hearing (Section V.B). OPR also found that victims were not treated with the "forthrightness and sensitivity" the Department expects (page xi-xii) but no professional misconduct on the victim-interaction issues; the DOJ statement repeats both findings.
- Page-label caveat: the standalone justice.gov Executive Summary PDF, as rendered by the fetcher, places its roman-numeral labels in an order that would put the two findings one page later (xi and xii) if labels are footers. The archive.org scan shows folios at the end of the page and puts them on x and xi. Two other prior readings split x/xi vs xi/xii. Check by eye in a PDF viewer before citing a page number; "Executive Summary, Section V.A and V.B" is safe.
- Body chapter page numbers (Chapter Two on the NPA, Chapter Three on victims): NOT VERIFIED. Sources tried: justice.gov PDF (summary only), archive.org OCR (offsets 0-840k only), theblackvault (13-page summary only), epstein-docs.github.io Bates pages 00023000 and 00023013 (title/cover only; 00023010-00023012 returned client errors), epstein-data.com case page (no page numbers).
