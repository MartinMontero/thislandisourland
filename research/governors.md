# 2026 Gubernatorial Elections — Fact Check of Claimed Winners (as of Nov 8, 2026)

Method: every item below was checked against pages retrieved live in this session. Wikipedia 2026 election pages (GA, FL, TX) were retrieved but were still pre-election snapshots with no results, so they were used only for candidate/endorsement background. Vote figures are unofficial and partial unless stated. All nine claimed winners did win, per retrieved sources. No race on this list is uncalled, though Wisconsin's AP call was not yet reported in one early source (see below).

Verdict key: CONFIRMED / CORRECTED (correct fact given) / UNVERIFIED (not found in retrieved sources) / FALSE.
