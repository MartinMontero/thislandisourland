The committee and candidate IDs were found through the API's own search; AIPAC PAC (C00797670) and United Democracy Project (C00799031) were confirmed through /committee/{id}/.

## Method notes and limits
- Schedule E rows appear twice, as 24/48-hour notices and as filed reports. The Stevens figures use the filed rows only; the notice rows total almost the same amount, so they are not added.
- Schedule A rows tagged as memo entries (memo code X) repeat earmarked gifts and were excluded.
- Only itemized contributions are visible. Small unitemized gifts are not.
- Only each candidate's principal committees were queried. Older or secondary committees may hold more.
- EDW "family" amounts for Gillen and Stevens include joint-fundraising transfers (receipt type 18G), not only direct PAC gifts.
- Secondary reports give different Stevens totals ($14.37M, about $10.5M, $15.9M). The FEC data here supports about $10.5M (Senate) plus about $3.9M (House, earlier cycles). The $15.9M figure was not reproduced.
- The records show who funded whom. They do not show that any contribution caused an absence.
