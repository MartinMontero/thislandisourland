# 2026 US House "historic firsts" and 2026 mayoral/local results — fact check (status as of Nov 8, 2026)

All verdicts below rest only on sources retrieved in this session. Verdict key: CONFIRMED / CORRECTED (correct fact given) / UNVERIFIED / FALSE.

## Part A — House claims: per-candidate verdicts

### Takeaway
All six Democrats won their November 3, 2026 races, according to the sources retrieved. Most of the "first" claims hold up. The details that need fixing: Kiros is a former lawyer, not a practising attorney. Hamawy is described as a "former" combat surgeon, and nothing retrieved says "retired." Benitez-Thompson's verifiable first is "first Democrat ever to win NV-02"; no source retrieved calls her the first Hispanic member from the district. NV-02 was an open seat because Amodei retired, so it was not "held by Amodei" on election day.
