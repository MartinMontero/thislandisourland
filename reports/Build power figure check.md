# Build power figure check, November 17, 2026

Method: each claim in the research attachment was checked against the original publisher where our tools could reach it (WebSearch returned publisher text for many sites). Most official organization sites were blocked from the shell and from subagents, so directory entries added in this pass are marked "Reported in 2026 sources" unless an official page or IRS data was read.

## Confirmed and used
- Ballotpedia: 2025, 68% of 38,609 tracked elections uncontested (26,318); 2024, 70% (53,485 of 76,902); 2024 state legislative seats, 2,227 of 5,807 (38%) without major-party competition; law-enforcement elections, 78% of 2,845.
- Run for Something: founded 2017; 1,600+ elected; pipeline over 250,000; 2025, 65% of November candidates won and 43 seats flipped (own statement, Feb. 1, 2026); 535 endorsed in 48 states for 2026 (its site).
- DSA: six DSA-endorsed House winners on Nov. 3, 2026 (NPR, citing AP); not a registered party; membership figures differ (120,000 claimed; 90,000 dues-paying per City & State).
- Working Families Party: ballot line in CT, NY, OR; active in 18 states (Guardian, Jan. 2026); full fusion only in NY and CT.
- DOE/Berkeley Lab (Dec. 2024): 176 TWh in 2023 (4.4%); 325 to 580 TWh by 2028 (6.7% to 12%).
- Grid Strategies / ACEG: 345 kV+ miles, nearly 4,000 in 2013; 2023 revised from 55 to about 450; 2024 revised from 322 to 888.
- McAlevey, Han and Engler book details and core arguments (publisher and review sources).

## Corrected
- "About 70% in 2020": 70% is 2024; 2020 was 64 to 65%.
- She Should Run founder: Erin Loos Cutraro, not the name given.
- Amy Gooch: a 2014 undergraduate capstone, not a journal article.
- Sister District is now States Win; Partnership for Working Families is now PowerSwitch Action.
- Co-governance guide has five practices and five pitfalls, not four practices.
- Emerge is for Democratic women; one report says state affiliates ended in December 2025.
- Run for Something: "500,000 offices" goal, "nearly 800 in 49 states" and "57/57/21" not found; age limit is 40.

### Browser verification pass (Nov. 17, 2026, Chrome)
Every directory entry that was not already confirmed was opened in a browser. 89 entries moved to Confirmed because the group's own page, or an official record, loaded and matched the entry; legal-form and tax details that were marked "not confirmed" stay marked that way. Final tally: 139 confirmed, 8 reported in 2026 sources, 2 not confirmed (Indivisible training link, Mutual Aid Hub).
Not confirmed, and why: Mutual Aid Hub, NLRB home page, Regulations.gov, Ballotpedia North Carolina filing guide, Washington State Republican Party PCO page (pages returned no readable text); Senate lobbying search (access denied to automated browsers); Marshall Ganz's site (blocked automated access, not bypassed).
Link problems: the Indivisible training link was an ended event; the Madison link redirects to a 2021 blog post; the Oklahoma Democratic Party /precinct link redirects to its home page; canivote.org redirects to a National Association of Secretaries of State page (entry now points there); the Open Collective docs page says it is out of date.
Exclusion-rule screening: no page read in this pass mentioned Stop the Steal, January 6, the Proud Boys, the Oath Keepers or Project 2025 other than critically. Trump appears on several pages only as criticism of or litigation against his administration. NACCHO, LegiScan, GovTrack, Congress.gov and LittleSis showed an automatic bot check that cleared by itself; nothing was bypassed.
