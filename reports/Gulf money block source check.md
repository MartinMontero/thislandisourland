# Source check finds three errors in gulf-money

Most of the block's factual claims hold up against the original documents, but about a dozen need correction before the site copy is treated as verified. Three are plain errors: the nuclear-agreement dates (the White House sent the agreement to Congress on August 24, 2026, not September 14; the Senate resolution was introduced October 15, not October 19; the House resolution September 16, not 17), the sentence that "Barrack was never made envoy" (he was named Special Envoy for Syria in May 2025), and the State inspector general finding, which covered the Secretary's emergency certification and not "the sales". Several more are overstated or imprecise: the Democratic hotel report's treatment of China, the Broidy and Nader "Oval Office contacts", the "minutes" detail in the New York Times account of the Affinity deal, "through about mid-2024" for the fee figures, the "management and ethics" gloss on State's Linick position, "calls the suit unserious", and the unverified "no reactor or nuclear material was transferred". The core framing (documented payments, no finding tying any payment to a decision, claims labelled by who made them) is not undermined by anything found. Every fetch behind these findings went through a summarising tool, so quotes and page numbers need a spot check against the raw documents before publication. Nothing in the "Saudi Trump financial ties check" folder was read or used.

## Verdict key and how far to trust it

CONFIRMED (primary) means the original document was opened and its wording, as relayed by the extraction tool, supports the claim. CONFIRMED-SECONDARY means only press or relay copies were read. CONTRADICTED means the source says something different from the block. NOT VERIFIED means no source opened supports or refutes it. All fetches went through WebFetch-type tools that return model-written extracts, not raw text, and direct downloads were blocked by the proxy (HTTP 403), so no PDF was checked with a text extractor. Page numbers cited below for the Oversight report are the extractor's and may be off by one or two. Short quotations are near-verbatim and should be checked against the documents before being used inside quotation marks on the site.

## Conclusion

The corrections that change meaning, not just precision, are the Barrack-envoy sentence, the inspector general sentence, the three nuclear-review dates, and the Broidy and Nader "Oval Office" phrasing, because each states something the source does not. The rest are attribution and wording fixes that make the block more defensible without changing its argument. Two caveats cut the other way: the block's "no court finding, inspector general report, indictment, plea or jury verdict" framing was not itself tested here (only the individual claims were), and several of its negatives (no Affinity reply, no DOJ finding, no reactor transfer) are absence of evidence from web searches, not documented absence.

Before publication, spot-check against the raw documents: the Oversight report pp. 10, 13, 46, 56, 69-70, 77-79 and 99-101; the exact sentences quoted from the two Wyden-related letters; the OIG "executed in accordance" quotation; the "does not show impropriety" sentence; and the Mnuchin and Tillerson quotations. Treat every page number and quotation in this report as extractor-reported until then.
